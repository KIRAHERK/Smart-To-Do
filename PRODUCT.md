# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Zakkirni (ذكّرني, "remind me"), formerly Smart To-Do, by Hussein Al Herk. Facts come from the README, docs/USER_GUIDE_ar.md and the owner's messages; audience and page language were confirmed by the owner.

## Platform

android

## Users

- **The app:** an older Arabic-speaking businessman who used to keep his daily and weekly lists on paper and throw them away. Not a typist; he speaks. Large text. Uses it at the office, in the car and at home. His phone is a Samsung Galaxy A9 (2018) on Android 10.
- **Anyone else who installs it:** Arabic speakers on Android 8+ who would rather talk than type. Each needs their own free Gemini API key.
- **The download page and README:** first, visitors from the developer's portfolio (employers, clients) judging the work; second, Arabic speakers who want to install it in a minute.

## Product Purpose

He says what he must do; the app writes it down, files it in a category, reminds him, asks how it went and keeps the whole record, so nothing is forgotten and he can ask "when did I last call Ahmed?".

## Positioning

A personal assistant's notebook you talk to in Arabic, any dialect. Every change is kept forever, nothing he says is lost (offline speech is queued and added by itself), and it runs on the free Gemini tier, so it costs nothing.

## Operating Context

Many short visits a day: glance at Today, press Talk, confirm, leave. Morning summary, evening review and weekly review arrive as notifications. Installed from GitHub Releases, then updates itself in the app without losing data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Voice first (big Talk button), confirmation card before saving (every detail editable), Work / Home / Personal / Travel plus custom categories, eight statuses plus "missed", reminders and repeats, notes by voice, History with status filter and multi-delete, WhatsApp-like assistant chat with play/pause read-aloud, offline queue, daily backups, in-app updates, first-run guide. Arabic RTL first, English available. Android 8.0+ (minSdk 26). Apache-2.0, open source. Free tier only: no paid services.

## Evidence on Hand

Real screenshots in docs/screenshots/ (today, listening, confirm, task_detail) and rendered by `ScreenshotTest`. No user count, reviews, testimonials or press: never invent any.

## Product Principles

- Never lose his words.
- Never surprise him: confirm before changing anything.
- Fewer, bigger things on screen.
- Free to run, honest about what it needs (a Gemini key, Android 8+).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large text, high contrast, 48dp touch targets, TalkBack labels, never color alone.
