Nothing gets lost.

Made for a businessman who wrote his lists on paper and threw them away. Now every task, note and change stays.

Every change is kept

Each status and note is dated. Search by voice or text, and filter History by status or category.

Works without internet

If you're offline, what you said is saved and added by itself as soon as Wi-Fi or data is back.

It asks how it went

A morning summary, an evening review and a Friday weekly review. Answer by voice and your tasks update.

No surprises

Nothing is saved or deleted without your yes, and any deleted task can be brought back.

Updates itself

It tells you about a new version and installs it over the old one, with a backup before every update and one every day.

Install in a minute.

Free and open source. It comes from the project's GitHub Releases, signed with the same key every time.

Download for Android
You'll need: an Android 8 phone or newer, and a free Gemini key from Google AI Studio (two minutes, no card needed).
  1. Download the APKOpen this page on the phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. Allow the installOpen the file and allow “Install unknown apps” when Android asks.
  3. Follow the setup listMicrophone, notifications, battery, then paste your Gemini key once.
  4. Press TalkSay your first task. Future updates arrive inside the app.
